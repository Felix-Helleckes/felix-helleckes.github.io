Felix Helleckes

Indie app developer & senior QA engineer · Cologne, Germany

I'm Felix Helleckes, a senior QA automation engineer with more than eight years of experience in web, mobile and IoT testing. In my spare time I build small, focused apps and ship them myself, from the first line of code to the store listing.

Most of my apps bring back a piece of 2000s technology in a modern form: a click-wheel music player, a walking RPG with grid battles, a retro desktop messenger and an LED world clock. I build mobile apps with Flutter and desktop apps with Electron.
