Retrogram

WhatsApp and Telegram in a retro desktop messenger · by Felix Helleckes

Retrogram brings WhatsApp and Telegram into a desktop messenger in the style of the early 2000s, with a separate chat window per contact and switchable skins. Free and open source.

WebsiteMac App StoreGitHub

Why I built it

Retrogram is a nostalgia project: the look and feel of a 2000s desktop messenger for the chats people use today.

Features

  • WhatsApp and Telegram in one app
  • A separate window for every conversation
  • Classic notification sound and three skins
  • Windows, macOS and Linux, open source on GitHub
