Apps by Felix Helleckes

Indie app developer and senior QA engineer from Cologne, Germany

I build small, focused apps in my spare time and ship them end to end, from the code to the store listing. Here is everything I have published, with links to the App Store, Google Play and each app's own website.
Alle Apps von Felix Helleckes aus Köln auf einen Blick.

Clickwheel

Offline music player with a real click wheel that rotates by angle and clicks audibly. Plays your own MP3, FLAC and AAC files – no streaming, no account, no ads.

Website App Store Google Play

Project P.E.T.

Fan-made walking RPG: your real-life steps charge real-time grid battles, with 370 chips to collect, co-op boss raids, tournaments and online duels. On phone and desktop.

Website App Store Google Play

Dash & Slash

One-handed neon action roguelite: drag and release to dash through enemies, tap to parry. Procedurally generated rooms, three bosses, 28 relics.

Website App Store Google Play

Moto-Log

Offline service logbook for motorcycles: maintenance entries, service intervals and running costs. No account, no trackers, five languages.

Website App Store Google Play

World Clock Live

LED world clock as a live desktop wallpaper for Windows and macOS – 24 cities, automatic daylight-saving time, near-zero CPU usage.

Website Mac App Store

Retrogram

WhatsApp and Telegram in one retro desktop messenger in the style of the 2000s – a window per contact and three skins. Free and open source.

Website Mac App Store GitHub

EU Compliance Suite

Shopify app for the EU withdrawal button, the legal-guarantee notice and the durability label, in 24 EU languages.
