EU Compliance Suite

Shopify app for the 2026 EU consumer-law duties · by Felix Helleckes

The EU Compliance Suite covers three 2026 EU duties for Shopify stores in one app: the withdrawal button, the harmonised legal-guarantee notice and the durability label, in 24 languages.

WebsiteShopify App Store

Why I built it

The app is built so that a store owner can switch on the new EU requirements without touching theme code.

Features

  • Withdrawal button according to Art. 11a CRD
  • Harmonised legal-guarantee notice
  • Durability label blocks, free forever
  • 24 EU languages, no theme code needed
