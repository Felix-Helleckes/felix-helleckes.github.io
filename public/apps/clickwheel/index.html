Clickwheel

Offline music player with a real click wheel · by Felix Helleckes

Clickwheel turns your phone into a dedicated music player that you operate with a click wheel, the way MP3 players worked in the 2000s. It plays the files that are on your device: no account, no streaming, no ads.

WebsiteApp StoreGoogle Play

Why I built it

I built Clickwheel because I missed listening to my own music without notifications, autoplay and a monthly bill. The wheel rotates by angle and clicks audibly, and you can try it right in the browser on the website before installing anything.

Features

  • Plays MP3, M4A/AAC, FLAC, WAV, AIFF, OGG and Opus from your device
  • Real click wheel: rotate to scroll, press the center to select
  • Works completely offline, the Android version has no internet permission
  • Folder view, playlists, tag manager and seven case colours
  • Free player, one-time purchase for the extras, no subscription

Questions

Is Clickwheel a subscription?

No. The player is free and has no ads. A one-time purchase of 4.99 € adds the tag manager, playlists and all seven case colours. Nothing renews or expires.

Can Clickwheel play Spotify or Apple Music tracks?

No. Streaming catalogues are protected, so no third-party app can play them. Clickwheel plays music files you own that are stored on your phone.

Which audio formats does it support?

MP3, M4A/AAC, FLAC, WAV, AIFF, OGG and Opus. Lossless files are played as they are, without re-encoding, equaliser or loudness adjustment.

Does Clickwheel collect data?

No. There are no analytics, ads, accounts or servers. The Android release does not even hold the INTERNET permission.

Can I try the click wheel before installing?

Yes. The website has a working click wheel you can turn in the browser.
